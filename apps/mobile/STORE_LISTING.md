## Full description

GoalSlot is a scheduling app built around a simple idea: goals don't get
done because you wrote them down, they get done because you put them on the
calendar. Instead of a to-do list you re-read every morning and re-order
every night, GoalSlot turns your goals into scheduled time — a day view that
shows exactly what you're doing and when, built from your goals, tasks, and
schedule together instead of three separate lists you have to reconcile
yourself.

Start each day with Today — a single agenda that pulls together what's
scheduled, what's due, and what's still open, so you're not hunting across
tabs to figure out what to work on next. Break goals into tasks, schedule
them against real time slots, and track how long things actually take with
the built-in time tracker. Everything works offline: log time, check off
tasks, and adjust your schedule without a connection, and it syncs the
moment you're back online.

GoalSlot is for anyone who's tired of planning systems that live apart from
the day they're supposed to organize — students blocking out study time,
freelancers tracking billable hours against project goals, or anyone who
wants their calendar and their to-do list to finally agree with each other.
